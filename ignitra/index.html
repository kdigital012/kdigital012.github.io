What is Ignitra?

Ignitra is a dietary supplement sold in capsule form, 30 capsules per bottle. The maker calls it a proprietary formula of 11+ plants, minerals and nutrients, made to support a healthy metabolism and weight management. According to the official website it is manufactured in the USA.

It is not a medicine, and it does not replace balanced meals, good sleep or movement. The maker also describes it as a natural formula with no stimulants that is non-habit forming. Those are the seller’s own statements, so read the full label before you order.

Quick verdict

Ignitra may suit adults who already work on diet and activity and want a daily capsule to support that routine. It is not a quick fix, results differ for everyone, and it is worth speaking to a doctor first, especially if you take medication.
